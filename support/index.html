Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. LumaLog works fully without one, and your workouts stay on your phone. An account backs them up and syncs them between your devices.

My confirmation or reset email hasn't arrived

Check your spam or junk folder. If it's there, marking it as "not spam" helps future emails arrive normally.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. You'll get a code by email to set a new one.

My workouts aren't showing on my other device

Sign in with the same account on both devices and make sure you're online. LumaLog syncs when you open the app and after you finish a workout.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the workouts stored with it.
